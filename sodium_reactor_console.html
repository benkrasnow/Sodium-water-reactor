Linear actuator

Force PID drives FWD/REV to hold the setpoint. Reset applies the fixed signed retract PWM until the distance target is reached, then holds. Manual drives the signed PWM directly (+ = FWD, − = REV).

Flow is interpolated from the measured 0.2 mm nozzle table. The pump delivers nothing below PWM 300 and plateaus at 14.3 ml/min from PWM 800 up, so the useful range is the 300–800 span; the slider still goes anywhere.

Disc speed is mechanical RPM; the firmware multiplies by the pole-pair count before sending to the VESC. 0 rpm releases the motor (coast).

Force PID tuning

g

The loop runs once per acquisition cycle (~9 Hz) — the HX711 cannot give accurate data faster than 10 Hz.

Reset (retract)

mm
ms

The retract PWM is signed and applied as-is: its sign picks the direction that retracts on this machine (negative drives REV). Check it in Manual mode first — the wrong sign drives into the junction instead of away from it, and only the timeout would stop it.
